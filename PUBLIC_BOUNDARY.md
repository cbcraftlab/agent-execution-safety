# Public Repository Boundary

The public repository is intentionally documentation-first.

Do publish:

- product positioning;
- safety workflow;
- scoring contract shape;
- harmless testset examples;
- redacted report examples;
- contribution and security boundaries.

Do not publish yet:

- private evaluator core;
- full adapter implementation;
- real Codex/Cursor traces;
- complete adversarial suite;
- bypass-oriented payload collections;
- internal local paths, credentials, tokens, or account data.
